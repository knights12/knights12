## BUDGIT: AN AFRICAN ORGANISATION USING DATA FOR SOCIAL IMPACT

**Country:** Nigeria, Africa  
**Founded:** 2011  
**Organisation:** BudgIT  
**Website:** [https://budgit.org](https://budgit.org)

### About the Organisation
BudgIT is a Nigerian civic-technology organisation established in 2011. It uses data, technology and citizen engagement to promote transparency, accountability and effective public service delivery. The organisation was created to make government financial information easier for ordinary citizens to understand and use. Although established in Nigeria, BudgIT has expanded its activities into other African countries.

###  What Does the Organisation Do with Data?
BudgIT collects, analyses and presents government financial and development project data. It works with information relating to public budgets, government expenditure, healthcare, education, infrastructure and community development.

Through its digital platforms, research publications, visual reports and community engagement programmes, it transforms complex government information into understandable and useful insights.

One of its major initiatives is **Tracka**, a project-monitoring platform launched in 2014. Tracka enables citizens to monitor government-funded projects in their communities, report abandoned or poorly implemented projects and demand accountability from relevant authorities.

###  What Changed Because of the Data?
BudgIT’s data-driven activities have contributed to positive social and developmental changes, including:

- **Electricity:** Its advocacy helped facilitate electricity access in Kawu community, Niger State, after approximately 30 years without electricity.
- **Healthcare:** Community monitoring and advocacy have supported improvements in public healthcare infrastructure.
- **Education:** Its project-monitoring activities have helped communities advocate for better school infrastructure.
- **Transparency:** Citizens have gained improved access to government budget and expenditure information.
- **Accountability:** Communities can use evidence to question government project implementation and demand action.

According to BudgIT’s 2024 annual report, the organisation tracked 1,404 capital projects across 25 states, profiled 195 primary healthcare centres in 13 states and facilitated 344 community meetings.

### Why Is the Organisation Important to Africa?
BudgIT demonstrates how data can be used beyond business and technology to address social challenges. Its approach encourages evidence-based decision-making, responsible public spending, citizen participation and improved governance. Its model also provides an example that other African countries can adapt to monitor public development projects and improve service delivery.

### Conclusion
BudgIT is an excellent example of an African organisation using data to create meaningful social impact. By collecting, analysing and sharing public information, it empowers citizens to participate in governance and hold public institutions accountable. Its work demonstrates that data becomes valuable when it is transformed into practical information that helps solve real-world problems.

**Key Lesson:** Data alone does not create change; its effective analysis, communication and application can improve communities and transform society.
